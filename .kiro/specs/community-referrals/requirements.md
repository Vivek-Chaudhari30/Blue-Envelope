# Community + Referrals — Requirements (EARS)

Sources: the user's Stage 2 brief, `.kiro/steering/community-safety.md`, `.kiro/specs/community-referrals/BACKLOG.md`. Minimum scope. All data synthetic / demo-labeled.

## Data & storage

**D1 — Adapter.** The system SHALL use Supabase WHEN `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` exist, otherwise a `CommunityStore` demo adapter with the same interface. Access is server-only.
**D2 — Towns.** `lib/community/towns.json` SHALL hold all 351 MA municipalities with county and 2020 population (from the MassGIS municipalities layer; generated by `scripts/load-towns.ts`, committed).
**D3 — Members.** A member record: `id` (random uuid, also in localStorage), `town_id` nullable, `language`, `helper_code` (short random unique), `referred_by_code` nullable, `display_name_optin` nullable, `created_at`.
**D4 — Events.** An event: `member_id`, `type ∈ {started, xray_done, packet_ready, marked_sent, contact_updated, referral_opened}`, `town_id`, `created_at`.
**D5 — Suppression.** Aggregation per town/county SHALL apply small-cell suppression (counts 1–9 → "fewer than 10") BEFORE anything leaves the server.
**D6 — Seed.** `scripts/seed-community.ts` SHALL create ~40 towns of labeled synthetic history, Lowell included.

## Counting (server-enforced — community-safety)

**C1.** A referral SHALL count only when the invitee reaches `packet_ready`.
**C2.** A referral SHALL NEVER count for a self-referral (same device / member id).
**C3.** A referral SHALL count at most once per invitee.
**C4.** At most 20 counted referrals per helper per day.
**C5.** Core help SHALL never be gated by referrals or badges.

## Questions screen

**Q1.** The Questions screen SHALL offer an optional searchable "Which town do you live in?" with a "Prefer not to say" option.

## Submit screen

**S1.** "I sent it to MassHealth" SHALL log a `marked_sent` event.
**S2.** A "Help a neighbor who got a blue envelope" card SHALL show: the link `/r/{helper_code}`, Copy link, Share on WhatsApp (prefilled message in all six languages), a toggle "Show my first name and town on the Community Board" (OFF by default), and "My impact" (neighbors-helped count + badges: Neighbor Helper 1, Community Builder 3, Envelope Ambassador 10).

## Referral landing (`/r/[code]`)

**R1.** `/r/[code]` SHALL set `referred_by_code`, log `referral_opened`, show "{FirstName} from {Town} shared this with you" only if the helper opted in (else "A neighbor shared this with you"), then go to Home. An invalid code SHALL go to Home quietly.

## Community Board (`/community`, public)

**B1.** Linked from Home and the flow rail. Public.
**B2.** A statewide progress bar toward 360,000, labeled "Actions in Blue Envelope. MassHealth records are not visible to us."
**B3.** A town ranking by neighbors helped per 1,000 residents, with a county filter.
**B4.** Opted-in helpers (first name + last initial + town).
**B5.** A "Demo data" label; refresh every 10 seconds; an accessible table version of the ranking.

## APIs (zod-validated, rate-limited)

**A1.** `POST /api/members`, `POST /api/events`, `GET /api/community` (aggregates only), `GET /api/referral/[code]`.
**A2.** No API response SHALL contain a member id, phone, address, or answer.

## Properties (fast-check)

- No `/api/community` or `/api/referral` response contains a member id.
- Every town/county count in 1–9 is suppressed ("fewer than 10").
- Town counts sum to the county and state totals (before suppression).
- A referral counts once per invitee; never for self; never more than 20/helper/day.
- Badge thresholds: Neighbor Helper at 1, Community Builder at 3, Envelope Ambassador at 10.
